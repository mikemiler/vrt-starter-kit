Our story

Two ovens, one stubborn idea.

Olive & Ash started in 1998 with a wood-fired oven and a sourdough starter that is older than most of our staff. We still bake every loaf by hand, starting at 3 in the morning.

Our flour comes from a mill 40 kilometres away. Our olives come from a family grove we visit every autumn. Nothing fancy, just time and good ingredients.
